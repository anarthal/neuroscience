oxidation: reaction where a species donates electrons
    The species becomes oxidized
    Oxidation number increases
reduction: reaction where a species accepts electrons
    The species becomes reduced
    Oxidation number decreases
oxidation number: the charge that each atom in a molecule/ion would have if all bonds were pure ionic
    E.g. in H2O, O has -2, H has +1
redox reaction: pair of oxidation and reduction reactions
    The element that gets oxidized reduces the other => reducing agent
    The element that gets reduced oxidizes the other => oxidizing agent
