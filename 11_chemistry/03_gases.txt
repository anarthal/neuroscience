VAPOR PRESSURE: for a liquid, pressure that the molecules that evaporate exert in equilibrium
    NORMAL BOILING POINT: T for which vapor pressure == P_atm
    Increases with temperature exponentially
    CLAUSIUS-CLAPEYRON EQUATION: relation between P_vapor and T
        ln(P) = -(H_vapor/RT) + C
        H_vapor: molar enthalpy of vaporization. Supposed to remain constant with T
        C: constant. Use the formula only to compute differences
Phase diagrams
    TRIPLE POINT: (P, T) point where 3 phases co-exist
    CRITICAL POINT: (P, T) point such that for higher P and T, the substance exists as a supercritical fluid
    SUPERCRITICAL FLUID: intermediate state between phase and gas, reached at very high P and T
Solution magnitudes
    MOLARITY: moles of solute by unit volume of solution
    MOLALITY: moles of solute by unit mass of solvent
    MOLAR FRACTION (χ): moles of solute by total moles of substance in the solution
HENRY'S LAW: solubility of a gas increases linearly with pressure
    Solubility (max molarity) = k_H * p
    k_H: constant that depends on the gas, the solvent and T
